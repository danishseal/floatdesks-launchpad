---
title: "Going Long"
description: "Buy synth on the launch curve or the AMM to get liquid exposure to a collectible market."
---

Going long on ***commas*** means holding a market's synth token. The token is a
funding-pegged synthetic: its price is pulled toward the oracle index by
arbitrage and funding, not locked to it. You are buying exposure to the
underlying's price in SOL terms, with a soft peg.

<Note>
Expect a basis of a few percent between the token price and the index. The
[funding mechanism](/concepts/funding) narrows persistent gaps over time; it
does not eliminate them block by block.
</Note>

## Where you trade depends on market phase

Each market goes through two phases:

1. **Bootstrap**: the token trades on a [bonding curve](/concepts/launch-curve).
   Buys draw tokens from the preminted curve allocation; sells return them to it.
2. **Live**: after [graduation](/concepts/graduation), trading moves to the
   market's internal [AMM](/concepts/amm), a constant-product pool seeded from
   the curve raise.

Both phases charge a per-trade fee set in the market's
[parameters](/concepts/parameters) (`curve_fee_bps` and `amm_fee_bps`).

## Buying on the curve

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the market status">
    Fetch the `Market` account. `status` must be `Bootstrap` and `frozen` must
    be false.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote and set slippage">
    Compute the expected output from the curve's virtual reserves, then pass a
    `min_tokens_out` you are willing to accept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send curve_buy">
    Your SOL goes to the market vault and the synth is transferred from the
    curve allocation to your associated token account (created automatically
    if needed).
  </Step>
</Steps>

```typescript
await program.methods
  .curveBuy(new BN(2 * LAMPORTS_PER_SOL), minTokensOut)
  .accountsPartial({
    market: marketPda,
    synthMint: mintPda,
    solVault: vaultPda,
    user: wallet.publicKey,
    userAta: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(mintPda, wallet.publicKey),
  })
  .rpc();
```

You can exit during bootstrap with `curveSell(tokensIn, minSolOut)`, which
returns your tokens to the curve allocation and pays out SOL, minus the curve
fee.

## Trading on the AMM

After graduation, use `ammBuy` and `ammSell` with the same slippage pattern.
The AMM adds one extra account, the market's pool token account:

```typescript
await program.methods
  .ammBuy(new BN(1 * LAMPORTS_PER_SOL), minTokensOut)
  .accountsPartial({
    market: marketPda,
    synthMint: mintPda,
    poolToken: poolPda,
    solVault: vaultPda,
    user: wallet.publicKey,
    userAta: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(mintPda, wallet.publicKey),
  })
  .rpc();
```

## Reading premium and basis

Two fields on the `Market` account tell you where the token trades relative to
the underlying:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `index_twap` | Oracle index, lamports per whole underlying unit |
| `mark_ema` | Smoothed AMM price, lamports per whole underlying unit |

The premium is `(mark_ema - index_twap) / index_twap`. A positive premium
means the token trades rich to the index; [funding](/concepts/funding) is then
paying shorts to draw and sell, which pushes the price back down. A negative
premium means the token trades cheap; shorts are being charged, which pushes
them to buy back and repay.

Remember the scale: 1,000,000 tokens represent one whole underlying unit (one
floor NFT or one graded card), and the token has 6 decimals.

<Warning>
A frozen market (circuit breaker or admin action) rejects all trades until it
is unfrozen. See [circuit breaker](/concepts/circuit-breaker). Positions and
balances are untouched while frozen; only activity is paused.
</Warning>
