---
title: "Buy a token"
description: "Pay with SOL, or deposit a real copy of the collectible."
---

There are two ways in: SOL, like any token, or the collectible itself.

## Pay with SOL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the market and pick an amount">
    The trade panel quotes exactly what you will receive, the 0.70% fee,
    and the price impact before you sign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    On the curve your buy moves the price along the launch curve; after
    migration it trades against the AMM pool. Either way the fill lands in
    one transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pay with the collectible

If you hold a registered on-chain copy of the underlying (a vaulted card NFT or a collection NFT), you can deposit it instead of paying SOL.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to 'Pay with the collectible'">
    The panel detects registered copies in your wallet and shows the item's
    live value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    You receive tokens **equal in value to the copy at the current price**:
    not a fixed amount. If the token trades cheap, one copy buys more
    tokens; if it trades rich, fewer. The copy goes into the market's
    escrow, where anyone exiting can later claim it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Item deposits work in both phases: on the curve from the very first
  block, and on the AMM after migration. Pricing uses a smoothed mark, so
  the rate cannot be manipulated inside a single transaction.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Paying with the collectible is feeless.** SOL trades pay the 0.70%
  fee; depositing a real copy pays nothing, by design: bringing the real
  thing into the pool is the behavior the protocol most wants to reward.
</Tip>
