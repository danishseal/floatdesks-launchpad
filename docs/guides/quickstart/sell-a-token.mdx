---
title: "Sell a token"
description: "Exit to SOL on the curve or the AMM."
---

Selling mirrors buying: same venues, same fee, quoted before you sign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an amount">
    The panel quotes the SOL you will receive after the 0.70% fee and shows
    the price impact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    During the curve phase your sell walks back down the launch curve.
    After migration it sells into the AMM pool, which started with 100 SOL
    of real liquidity at migration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exiting into the real thing

If the market's escrow holds deposited copies of the collectible, you have a second exit: instead of selling for SOL, redeem your tokens for an actual copy. See [Take out the card](/guides/quickstart/redeem-a-card).

<Note>
  Selling is never gated: no lockups, no windows, no allowlists. The curve
  and the AMM are live around the clock.
</Note>
